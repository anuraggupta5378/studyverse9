Resource Workflow

1. Select curriculum
Choose the subject, part and chapter where this resource belongs.

2. Add resource information
Give the resource a clear title and select its learning category.

3. Attach files
PDFs, pictures and video files can be selected directly from your phone or computer.

4. Save draft
Keep the resource unpublished while you continue working.

5. Submit for review
A Manager/Admin can review and publish the resource.

Supported files

📄 PDF
🖼️ JPG, PNG, WEBP and other browser-supported images
🎬 MP4 and other browser-supported videos
